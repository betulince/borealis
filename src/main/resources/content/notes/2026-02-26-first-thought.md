The best way to learn is to build. Not just follow tutorials, but actually create something that solves a problem or brings you joy.

This website is a perfect example - I could have used a static site generator or a CMS, but building it from scratch taught me so much more about:

- Design systems and theming
- Content management strategies
- The value of simplicity

Sometimes the journey is more valuable than the destination.
